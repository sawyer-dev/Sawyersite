## Overview

The D&D Character Android App lets players of the tabletop RPG Dungeons & Dragons track their characters' stats, traits, abilities, spells, inventory, and notes on an Android phone. There is no need to remember to bring a paper character sheet and notebook.

## Stack

Built in Android Studio with Kotlin and XML layouts.
