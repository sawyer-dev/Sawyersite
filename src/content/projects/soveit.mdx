## Overview

SoVeIt is a virtual reality stealth driving game. You drive around the city of Parkov searching for muffins while avoiding the drones of the Soviet "Wolves", and you win by returning the muffins to Grandma's House.

It was created by Husky Game Development Enterprise, Fall 2019 and Spring 2020, Team 4, also known as Team VeR.
