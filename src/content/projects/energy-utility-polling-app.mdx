## Overview

This web application is meant for both education and data collection. It gives customers short descriptions of the utility programs available in their location, then uses polling questions to ask what utilities their household uses now and whether that would change given the new information.

The answers give the partner utilities information for future decisions, and help show how public utilities in Michigan can most effectively move to renewable energy and more efficient energy use.
