## Overview

D&D VR Dungeon Drop (Dungeon Drop for short) is an immersive gallery of virtual fantasy encounters. Each one is a fully 3D, animated, interactable scene, and together they feature four iconic monsters from Dungeons & Dragons.

It was my final project for CS5641, Immersive Virtual Environments, in spring 2021.

## How it plays

While wearing an Oculus headset, you face the first encounter. Every encounter has a grabbable sword you can interact with, though it does nothing more than that. The monster in each scene loops through animations to bring it to life. You can press the A button on the right controller to change the scene every 3 seconds.
