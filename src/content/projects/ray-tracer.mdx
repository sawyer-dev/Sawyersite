## Overview

This ray tracer was developed for my Graphics class in the fall 2020 semester. It is written in C and uses precise mathematical formulas to shoot rays into a scene, detect the shapes they hit, and draw each shape according to its location, color, and position relative to the light source. It can also render reflective surfaces and shadows.
