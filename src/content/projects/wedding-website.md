## Overview

I designed and deployed my wedding website as a full-stack web application. It used Angular on the front end and Google Apps Script with Google Sheets as a lightweight backend, supporting interactive experiences for the day of the event.

## Stack

- **Front end:** Angular and TypeScript.
- **Backend:** Google Apps Script, with Google Sheets as the data store.
- **Scope:** Interactive day-of event features.
