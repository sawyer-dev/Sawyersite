## Overview

Cards of the Ancient Zoo is a 2D-style RPG and card game hybrid. Exploration happens in the RPG overworld, and battles are played as card games. Short fetch-quests and puzzles in the overworld reward you with new cards to build into your own deck, and NPCs share the lore of the world and hint at hidden discoveries.

It was created by Husky Game Development Enterprise, Spring 2022 Team 1.

## My contributions

- **Team leadership:** led an Agile team through sprint planning, task coordination, and milestone delivery.
- **Gameplay systems:** designed data-driven systems for card management and enemy strategy.
- **UI architecture:** designed the structure of the game's user interface.
- Built with C# and Unity.
