## Overview

Salad Siege is a virtual reality tower-defense game. You defeat angry vegetables by placing army-man towers and ranch bottles around the map to keep them from reaching your plate. Survive all three levels to win.

It was created by Husky Game Development Enterprise, Fall 2020 and Spring 2021, Team 4, also known as Team VeR.
