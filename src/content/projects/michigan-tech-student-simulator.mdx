## Overview

Michigan Tech Student Simulator is a simple platformer developed for the Michigan Tech Winter Wonder Hack 2020. You play as Blizzard, the Michigan Tech mascot, and travel through the MEEM building to take your exam.

It was created by Winter Wonder Hack Team Mild Sauce.
