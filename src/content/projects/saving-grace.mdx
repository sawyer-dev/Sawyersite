## Overview

Saving Grace is an adventurous turn-based role-playing game. You play as Sawyer from Michigan Tech and travel to the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor to save Grace from quarantine.

It was created for Wolverine Shammy Jam 2020 by Team Sauce.
