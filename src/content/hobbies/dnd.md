## What it is
D&D is one of many table-top role playing games where players control a single character that they created, forming a party of adventurers who take on epic quests, dive into dungeons, or simply fool around in a fantasy setting created by the Dungeon Master, a player who acts as the referee and non-player-chracters, in order to level up and collaboratively tell an engaging, personalized story.

## How I got into it
My dad had played and ran the early editions of D&D in college, and after discovering some of his old books and notes, I decided to get 5th edition and run a campaign for my friends and I.

I have now ran 6 campaigns and am currently running my 7th.

## Where it is going
I am starting to branch out into different TTRPG systems, and am currently running my 7th campaign using a new system called Daggerheart.
My campaigns tend to run about a year long. After this campaign ends, I plan to try some shorter games in other systems before starting up an 8th campaign using Daggerheart again.
